What we do

How the work takes place.

01

Connect people with purpose

We bring together people from all backgrounds, professions, and walks of life who share a heart for serving others.

02

Put skills & gifts to work

From healthcare and emergency services to construction, education, ministry, trades, and everyday talents, we help people use what they know and what they love to make a difference.

03

Serve alongside local communities

We partner with trusted organizations and community leaders to support the work already being done and respond to genuine needs.

Meet practical needs

Depending on the mission, service may include medical outreach, community projects, food distribution, construction, education, disaster support, or simply being present where help is needed.

Our story

Sometimes you leave home believing you are going to change someone else's life, only to discover that God used the experience to change yours. Kristen & Ryan Forsythe — Founders

As a nurse and firefighter, we have both dedicated our careers to serving people during some of the most difficult moments of their lives. But over the years, God placed an even greater calling on our hearts: to use the skills and experiences He has given us beyond the walls of our hospitals, fire stations, and local communities.

Our journey into missions began together in 2018 in Tipitapa, Nicaragua. We arrived prepared to work and serve others, but we could never have anticipated how deeply that experience would change us in return.

We are especially passionate about creating mission opportunities for nurses, firefighters, paramedics, law enforcement officers, hospital staff, and others who spend their lives serving people — those who carry experiences and burdens that are difficult to explain to someone who hasn't lived them.

Why it matters

We go to serve. We return changed.

01

Everyone deserves to feel seen

Sometimes the greatest impact isn't what we build or provide — it's showing someone they are seen, valued, loved, and not forgotten.

02

Our gifts have a greater purpose

Our professions, skills, talents, and experiences become powerful tools when we choose to use them in service to others.

03

Relationships create lasting change

Mission work is more than completing a project. It's about listening, learning, serving alongside others, and building genuine relationships rooted in dignity and respect.

04

Service changes both sides

We may leave home hoping to make a difference in someone else's life, only to discover the experience transforms our own hearts, perspectives, and priorities.

05

Faith is meant to be lived

We believe following Christ means putting love into action — showing compassion, meeting people where they are, and serving without expecting anything in return.

06

We are better together

Different backgrounds, professions, talents, and experiences allow us to accomplish far more together than we ever could alone.

07

One experience can inspire a lifetime of service

A mission may last only days, but its impact can last a lifetime — encouraging people to continue serving in their homes, workplaces, churches, and communities.

When faith moves us beyond ourselves, service becomes more than something we do — it becomes part of who we are.
