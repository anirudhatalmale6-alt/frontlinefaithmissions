Our story

A nurse, a firefighter, and a trip that changed everything.

Frontline Faith Missions exists because two people who carry other people for a living found out what happens when someone carries them.

“Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others, as faithful stewards of God’s grace.”
1 Peter 4:10

The founders

Through these trips and a renewed faith, they were able to save and strengthen their marriage, build lifelong friendships, and turn their internal struggles into something purposeful. Kristen & Ryan Forsythe — Founders

Frontline Faith Missions was founded by Kristen and Ryan Forsythe, a nurse and a firefighter who have experienced firsthand the physical, emotional and spiritual demands of life on the front lines. In 2018 they were first introduced to short-term mission trips — an experience that would profoundly shape their lives.

Inspired by this transformation, Kristen and Ryan felt called to create a space where others in similar roles could experience the same kind of healing and growth.

Our mission

Give back. Go deeper. Come home different.

Our mission is to give first responders and hospital staff an opportunity to give back while deepening their walk in faith — or discovering a faith they may have never known. We believe that through serving others, engaging in meaningful group discussions, and praying together, true healing can take place in both the heart and mind.

We are especially passionate about supporting those dealing with PTSD and the unseen burdens that often come with serving others. Frontline Faith Missions exists to provide a safe, welcoming environment where individuals can share openly, find encouragement, and grow spiritually alongside a supportive community.

At our core, we are committed to helping our brothers and sisters in Christ find restoration, renewed purpose, and lasting hope through faith, service, and connection.
